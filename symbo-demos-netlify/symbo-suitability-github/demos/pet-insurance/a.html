Questions pet parents ask

Plain answers, before you decide.

How much does it cost?

Around ₹11,000 for 3 years, paid once. That is about ₹10 a day. The exact premium depends on your age, your pet's health and any add-ons you pick, and you see it before you pay.

Is my pet eligible?

Dogs and cats of every breed can join from 3 months old. There is no upper age limit.

My pet already has a health condition. Can I still buy?

Yes, you can apply. Most existing conditions are covered after a waiting period, usually 36 months, or 12 months for some conditions with the add-on. Some serious or terminal conditions cannot be insured. We ask about it and confirm before you pay.

How does cashless treatment work?

At a network hospital, show your policy ID card and your pet's vaccination card. The hospital sends the bill for covered treatment to the insurer and is paid directly.

What if there is no network hospital near me?

Use any vet. Pay the bill, then send the bills and reports online. The claim is paid to your bank account.

What documents do I need?

Your pet's vaccination card, front and back, and a quick KYC for you.

Who is the insurer?

The policy is underwritten by Bajaj General Insurance Limited, under its My Family Complete product.
