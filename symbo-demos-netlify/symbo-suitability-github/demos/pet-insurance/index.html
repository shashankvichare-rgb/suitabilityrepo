Your responsibilities as a pet parent

“Ongoing Pet Care Obligations (this will affect claim payments if not met or petcare is neglected)”

Yearly vaccines. Dogs: Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Bordetella (Kennel Cough), Leptospirosis.
